02

AR Banned Books

As the lead developer for the AR Banned Books Project, I created an interactive experience aimed at raising awareness about the importance of literary freedom. This project was showcased during Banned Books Week in the Memorial Union at Fort Hays State University, where it attracted over 500 visitors during it's week long run.
Using augmented reality, I transformed a physical bookshelf into a dynamic exhibit showcasing 30 banned or challenged books. Users were able to interct with the AR experience through the provided devices, revealing the titles of the banned books and their reasons for cencorship. This engaging approach allowed for participants to see a visual contrast from the virtual, full bookshelf in comparision to the empty physical bookshelf behind it.

Unity C# Sketchup Vuforia
